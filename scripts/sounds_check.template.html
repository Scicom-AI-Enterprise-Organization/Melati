Melati Sound Check

These are the AudioSet sound tags the pilot wrote into the transcripts of podcast recordings ( hours): tags in rows. Each clip is the row's own audio, from 3 s before the tag to 7 s after it; the bar under the player marks the tag (the line) and how long the models hear the sound (the shaded part).

  • RightYou hear that sound at the tag, or starting there.
  • WrongThat sound is not there.
  • Can't tellToo faint, or masked by the speech.
